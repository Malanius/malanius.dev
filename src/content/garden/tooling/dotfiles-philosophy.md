Rules I try to hold myself to: every config change goes through the repo, no snowflake edits on the machine itself. Everything has been managed as a [[nix-flakes]] repo since march, which makes the rule enforceable instead of aspirational.

The deeper philosophy: dotfiles are a garden, not a product. They're never done, and that's fine — the point is that tending them teaches you your tools.
