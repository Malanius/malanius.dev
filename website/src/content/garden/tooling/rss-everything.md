Ongoing project: route every feed-shaped thing in my life through the self-hosted reader. Blogs and release notes were easy. The good stuff is the derived feeds:

- GitHub releases for tools I pin in [[nix-flakes]]
- Anime airing schedules, scraped into RSS by a small worker
- Mangadex updates for the two series I follow ongoing

Everything unread in one inbox, no algorithmic resorting. The open web still works if you hold it right.
