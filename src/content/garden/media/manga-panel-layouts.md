Collecting vocabulary for why some pages read effortlessly and others fight you. Current notes:

Gutter width is pacing — wide gutters slow the eye, cramped ones accelerate. A bleed panel isn't "big for drama," it's a held breath; the story pauses while the image expands past time. Dungeon Meshi does dense 8-panel grids for comedy and then spends a full page on a single meal, and the contrast *is* the joke.
