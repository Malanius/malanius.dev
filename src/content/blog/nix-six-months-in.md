---
title: "Nix, six months in: a love-hate letter"
date: 2026-06-28
tags: [nix, tooling]
---

Six months ago I nuked my perfectly functional Arch install and rebuilt everything on NixOS. This is the retrospective I promised myself I'd write.

The love: my entire system is a git repo. New machine? `nixos-rebuild switch --flake .` and twenty minutes later everything is exactly where I left it, down to the terminal padding. Rollbacks have saved me twice from my own enthusiasm.

The hate: the documentation situation is still "read the source and squint," and error messages appear to be generated by an adversarial model. I keep a growing note of incantations in the garden because I will absolutely not remember them.

> Nix is what happens when you solve a real problem so thoroughly that using the solution becomes its own hobby.

Verdict: staying. But I understand every person who bounced off it. <span class="kaomoji">(￣ヘ￣)</span>
