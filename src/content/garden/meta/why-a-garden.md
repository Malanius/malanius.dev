A blog demands finished thoughts on a schedule. A garden lets notes exist at whatever stage they've actually reached — see the 🌱/🌿/🌲 stages on every page here.

The honest reason this exists: I kept losing my own learning. Things I understood in march were gone by june. Now they're planted, linked, and revisitable. Publishing them is almost incidental — see [[writing-in-public]] for that half of the argument.
