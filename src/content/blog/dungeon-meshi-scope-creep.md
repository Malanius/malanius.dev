---
title: What Dungeon Meshi taught me about scope creep
date: 2026-03-17
category: musings
tags: [manga, meta]
---

Laios and party set out to rescue one person. By volume nine they're renegotiating the fundamental relationship between the dungeon's ecosystem and the surface world. This is the most accurate depiction of a side project I have ever seen.

Every project I start is "eat monsters to save Falin": a clear goal, a scrappy plan, and a budget of zero. Every project I'm still working on six months later has somehow become "restructure the dungeon."

The lesson isn't "don't expand scope." Senshi's whole philosophy is that you engage seriously with what's in front of you, and the party is better for it. The lesson is to expand scope *deliberately*, one meal at a time, and to know which quest you're actually on.

Anyway, my anime notifier CLI now has a plugin system. I am aware of the irony.
