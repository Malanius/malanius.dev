Half-formed thought: the best games teach their systems the way good mentors teach — by arranging situations, not by explaining. World 1-1 is the canonical example, but Hades II does it continuously: every new boon combination is a tiny lesson plan you opted into.

Question I'm chewing on: what's the software-documentation equivalent of level design? Tutorials explain; playgrounds arrange situations.
