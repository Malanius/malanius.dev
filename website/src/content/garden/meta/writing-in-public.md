Working theory: publishing rough notes changes how you think more than how you write. Knowing a stranger might read [[nix-flakes]] makes me define terms I'd otherwise hand-wave, and the hand-waving is usually where I was wrong.

Fear to get over: someone knowledgeable reading a 🌱 note and judging it as a finished take. Mitigation: the growth stages exist precisely to set that expectation. We'll see if it works.
